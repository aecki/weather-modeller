INSIDE THE LAB

From a rain gauge to the bigger picture.

Follow official observations through transparent calculations, then explore a fitted extreme-value model with visible uncertainty.

01

Start with observations.

MeteoSwiss measures rainfall at weather stations. FOEN measures river discharge. This snapshot includes 18 weather stations, six river gauges, and weather records from 1981–2025. It is a selected sample of Switzerland.

02

Choose a window, add the rain.

The 1-, 3-, and 5-day controls add daily rainfall ending on your selected date. A rainfall day is 06:00 UTC to 06:00 the next day. A missing reading makes the whole total missing; zero means a measured dry day.

03

Read the map and river response.

Each map circle is a measurement at a station, not a storm footprint. The river line averages 24 hourly means over a matching window. Rainfall and river stations are independent selections, not assumed catchment pairs.

04

Put an event in context.

We compare its wettest window with the largest windows in complete calendar years. These historical counts are descriptive. The Model lab separately fits annual maxima to estimate return periods with sampling uncertainty. It does not estimate flood depth or insured loss.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Real records. Visible assumptions.

This explorer bundles a dated snapshot of official observations. It is not a live warning service, a flood-inundation model, or an insured-loss model.

Rainfall · MeteoSwiss

18 selected SwissMetNet stations; daily precipitation parameter rre150d0, in millimetres, 1981–2025. This is a sampled network, not all Swiss stations. A rainfall day runs from 06:00 UTC on the labelled date to 06:00 UTC the next day. Original series are not homogenised climate-trend records.

Rivers · FOEN

Six selected gauges, hourly mean discharge in m³/s, for the three replay windows. We compute a 06:00–06:00 UTC mean only when all 24 hourly means exist, using the API’s documented interval-start timestamps. Means are not instantaneous flood peaks. Original hourly records are included in the snapshot. Source release flags are preserved in the data and exports: 1 provisional, 2 validated, 3 final/replaced.

Calculations & missing values

3- and 5-day rainfall are trailing sums ending on the cursor date, including days before a replay window where needed. Any missing day makes that sum unavailable; missing values are never replaced with zero. The event maximum is the largest complete trailing window ending inside the replay. Coverage reports the underlying daily values in that replay.

Historical rankings compare annual maxima from complete calendar years only. Multi-day windows must fall entirely within that year. The Model lab separately fits stationary GEV and Gumbel distributions and reports bootstrap uncertainty. These are estimates. No flood-depth model, damage estimate or synthetic storm catalogue is included.

Rainfall points show measurements at stations; coloured circles are not storm footprints. River and rainfall charts use matching 06:00–06:00 UTC windows but do not establish a causal or catchment relationship.
